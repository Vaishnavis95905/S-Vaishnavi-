About

I started B.Tech IT not knowing what half the words on the syllabus meant. A few months in, I know a few more, and I like the feeling of being the slowest person in the room who still gets it in the end.

I'm the kind of learner who has to break things to understand them. My first programs mostly crashed, and each crash taught me more than the lecture before it did.

What pulls me towards technology is how a few lines of text can end up as something someone else can click, use and be annoyed by. I want to be good at making things people actually use, and this site is my first honest attempt at showing my work.
